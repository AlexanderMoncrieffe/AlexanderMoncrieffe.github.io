Creative Practice

Alongside his studio practice, Moncrieffe develops creative projects that explore the relationship between art, education and community.

Through workshops, artist-led projects and creative learning experiences, these practices extend painting beyond the studio and into shared spaces.

Discovering the Talent of the Future

Artist-in-residence programmes have provided opportunities to discover some truly remarkable young artists. Given the time, encouragement and artistic nourishment to develop, young people can begin to discover not only their own creative abilities, but the many different ways in which those abilities can be expressed.

Creativity does not always reveal itself immediately. Sometimes it needs space, patience and the freedom to experiment. Through painting, drawing, making and working alongside an artist, young people are given the opportunity to explore ideas, develop confidence and discover possibilities that may otherwise remain hidden.

For me, this is one of the most rewarding aspects of working within schools and communities: seeing a young artist begin to realise that they have something of their own to say.
